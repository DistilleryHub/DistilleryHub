Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] — replace before publishing

DistilleryHub ("we", "us") operates a professional networking platform for distillery, ethanol, laboratory, and plant professionals. This policy explains what information we collect through the app and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address or phone number, and a password hash (via Firebase Authentication) or Google sign-in identifier.
  • Profile information: headline, company, location, bio, profile photo, and — if you choose to upload one — your resume/CV.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, job listings, articles, marketplace listings, videos, status updates, and files you upload.
  • Messages and calls: chat messages (including images, videos, documents, and voice notes) sent through the app, and metadata about voice/video calls (participants, duration, timestamps) — not call audio/video content itself, which is transmitted peer-to-peer and not stored by us.
  • Usage and presence data: online/offline status, last-seen timestamps, and basic interaction data (likes, saves, applications, poll votes).
  • Device information: a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver notifications to your device.

How we use this information

  • To operate core features: authentication, the social feed, messaging, calling, job postings, and the marketplace.
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (see in-app Notification Settings).
  • To keep the platform safe — reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms of Service, and preventing abuse or spam.

Who can see your information

  • Your profile, posts, jobs, and articles are visible to other signed-in members (jobs and articles are also visible publicly and indexed by search engines, since they're designed to be discoverable).
  • Direct messages and group chats are visible only to the members of that conversation.
  • Your resume, if uploaded, is visible on your profile to other members and is shared with a job poster when you apply to their listing.

Third-party services we use

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, and push notifications.
  • Cloudinary: image, video, and document hosting for content you upload.
  • [If added] Sentry: error monitoring, to help us find and fix bugs.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Edit Profile → Danger Zone. This removes your profile and signs you out; content you created (posts, jobs, articles) remains visible but is attributed to a "removed member" rather than your name.

Your choices

  • Control which push notifications you receive in Notification Settings.
  • Block other members to stop seeing their content and prevent them from messaging you.
  • Mute individual chats to stop notifications from a specific conversation without leaving it.

Children's privacy

DistilleryHub is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
