# Privacy Policy — DistilleryHub

**Last updated:** [DATE]

DistilleryHub ("we", "us", "the app") is a professional networking app for
distillery, ethanol, laboratory, and plant professionals. This policy
explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information:** name, email address, professional headline,
company, and profile photo, when you sign up (email/password or Google
Sign-In).

**Content you create:** posts, comments, likes, shares, status updates,
articles, job listings, marketplace listings and RFQs/bids, group posts,
and files you upload.

**Communications:** direct and group chat messages, voice/video call
metadata (who called whom, and when — we do not record or store the
audio/video content of calls itself), and scheduled messages.

**Media uploads:** photos and videos you upload are stored with our
media provider (Cloudinary), not on your device or our servers directly.

**Usage data:** which connections you have, groups you've joined, and
basic activity needed to run features like notifications and unread
counts.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To operate core features: your feed, chat, calls, groups, jobs,
  marketplace, and notifications.
- To show your profile and posts to other users on the network, as
  intended for a professional networking app.
- To enforce our Terms of Service (e.g., handling blocked users and
  admin moderation of reported content).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Who Can See Your Information

- Your profile (name, headline, company, photo) is visible to other
  signed-in users of the app.
- Posts, comments, and status updates are visible per the visibility
  rules of that feature (e.g., group posts are visible to group
  members).
- Direct messages and calls are only visible/accessible to the
  participants in that conversation.
- Admins can view reported content for moderation purposes.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to run the app:

- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication, database (Firestore), and
  hosting infrastructure.
- **Cloudinary** — storage and delivery of uploaded photos and videos.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country as
part of providing their service. We do not control their infrastructure
directly but choose providers with industry-standard security practices.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your content (posts, messages, profile) is retained as long as your
account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data
deleted, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will remove it within
[TIMEFRAME, e.g. 30 days].

## 6. Security

We use Firebase's authentication and Firestore security rules to
restrict access to your data — for example, only chat participants can
read a conversation's messages, and only you can edit your own profile.
No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

DistilleryHub is intended for working professionals and is not directed
at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).
We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be
noted with an updated "Last updated" date above.
